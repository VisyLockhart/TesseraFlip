# TesseraFlip privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

TesseraFlip does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- **No network requests.** The extension never sends data anywhere. It has no server, no analytics, and no third-party code.
- **What it reads.** It runs only on `https://www.youtube.com/*`. It reads the page's video element so it can rotate it, step through frames, and capture the current frame. Captured frames are created locally, in your browser, and saved or copied only when you press the button or the shortcut.
- **What it stores.** Two settings are saved on your device with `chrome.storage.local`: the frame-step multiplier and the panel opacity. They never leave your computer and are removed when you uninstall the extension.
- **Not affiliated.** TesseraFlip is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/VisyLockhart/TesseraFlip/issues
