# Screenshot shot list

The store requires real screenshots of the extension, so these are taken by hand.
Size: 1280x800 (or 640x400), PNG or JPEG, no transparency. Up to 5.

## Setup

1. Use a video you may show publicly. "Big Buck Bunny" by the Blender Foundation (CC BY 3.0) works: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aqz-KE-bpKQ. Avoid music videos and other clips you do not have rights to.
2. In Chrome, open DevTools (F12), turn on the device toolbar (Ctrl+Shift+M), choose "Responsive", and set 1280 x 800.
3. Reload the extension, reload the page, open the player (a clean window with no other extensions' buttons helps).
4. Capture with the DevTools menu: `Ctrl+Shift+P`, then "Capture screenshot" (viewport only).

## Shots

1. **Panel on a paused frame.** Pause, open the panel, choose x2. Shows the button, the panel, and frame stepping.
2. **Rotated 90°.** Rotate right once with the panel open. Shows the video turned and fitted in the player.
3. **Opacity slider.** Drag the slider to about 50 while holding the mouse, so the value bubble is visible and the video shows through.
4. **Control-bar button close-up.** Panel closed, controls visible, mouse over the TesseraFlip button.
5. **Saved frame.** Open a saved PNG next to the player (a rotated frame makes the point that the file matches the screen).

Add a one-line caption for each in the store dashboard, for example "Step through frames one at a time" and "Rotate in 90° steps".
